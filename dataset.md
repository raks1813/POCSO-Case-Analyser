# Dataset and Case Compilation

The dataset used for this research consists of multiple High Court judgments concerning offences under the POCSO Act.

Each case has been systematically categorized based on:

- section invoked
- factual circumstances
- evidence presented
- judicial reasoning
- final verdict

## Case Categories

The dataset includes cases relating to:

- penetrative sexual assault
- aggravated penetrative sexual assault
- sexual assault

The dataset forms the empirical basis for analysing judicial trends and outcome patterns.
