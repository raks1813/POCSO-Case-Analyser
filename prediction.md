# Case Prediction Framework

This research project also explores the possibility of predicting case outcomes based on identified judicial patterns.

## Prediction Inputs

Potential prediction factors include:

- age of victim
- type of assault
- presence of medical evidence
- consistency of victim statement
- delay in complaint
- corroborative testimony

## Objective

The purpose of the prediction model is not to replace judicial discretion but to assist in understanding how certain factual patterns influence judicial outcomes.

This tool is intended strictly for **academic research and legal analysis.**
