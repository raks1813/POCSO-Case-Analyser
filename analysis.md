# Judicial Analysis

The analysis section examines how courts interpret evidence and statutory provisions in POCSO cases.

## Key Observations

Judicial reasoning frequently focuses on:

- consistency of victim testimony
- corroboration through medical evidence
- reliability of witnesses
- surrounding circumstances

Courts also consider the presence of aggravating factors such as:

- abuse of authority
- age of the victim
- repeated assault
- relationship between accused and victim

Through comparative examination of cases, patterns in judicial reasoning become identifiable.
