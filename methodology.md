# Research Methodology

The methodology adopted for this project involves qualitative legal analysis combined with structured case comparison.

## Case Selection

Judgments were selected from High Court decisions involving offences under the POCSO Act.

## Analytical Parameters

Each case was examined based on the following parameters:

- statutory provisions invoked
- factual background
- evidentiary strength
- victim testimony
- medical and forensic evidence
- judicial interpretation
- final outcome

## Analytical Approach

The study identifies recurring judicial patterns to determine how courts evaluate:

- credibility of witnesses
- evidentiary inconsistencies
- aggravating circumstances
- sentencing justification

This structured analysis forms the basis for developing a prediction framework.
