# About the Project

The **POCSO Case Analysis and Prediction Project** is an academic initiative aimed at analysing judicial decisions under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act.

The study focuses on identifying patterns in judicial reasoning while interpreting the statutory provisions relating to sexual offences against children.

The project examines factors such as:

- age of victim
- nature of allegations
- medical evidence
- witness credibility
- delay in FIR
- contradictions in testimony
- corroborative evidence

Through systematic examination of judgments, the project seeks to understand how courts determine liability and punishment.

This platform serves as a **legal research repository and analytical tool** for understanding judicial decision-making in POCSO cases.
